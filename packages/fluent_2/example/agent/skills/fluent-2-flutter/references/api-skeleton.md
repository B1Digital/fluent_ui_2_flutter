### `FluentSkeletonPainter`

Paints a skeleton: the stencil surface, plus the highlight band clipped to it.

Source: `packages/fluent_2/lib/src/surfaces/skeleton.dart`

#### Constructor: `FluentSkeletonPainter`

```dart
const FluentSkeletonPainter({
    required this.backgroundColor,
    required this.waveColor,
    required this.borderRadius,
    required this.bandWidthFactor,
    required this.bandOffsetFactor,
    required this.bandStops,
  });
```

| Field | Type | Required | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `backgroundColor` | `Color` | Yes | — | The stencil surface. `neutralStencil1`. |
| `waveColor` | `Color?` | Yes | — | The highlight the band carries. `neutralStencil2`, or null to paint no band at all — which is what a pulse skeleton does. |
| `borderRadius` | `BorderRadius` | Yes | — | Corner radius. The band is clipped to it, so a circular skeleton shimmers inside its own outline. |
| `bandWidthFactor` | `double` | Yes | — | The band's width as a multiple of the surface's. 3 while sweeping, 1 while still. |
| `bandOffsetFactor` | `double` | Yes | — | The band's left edge as a multiple of the surface's width. See [fluentSkeletonWaveOffset]. |
| `bandStops` | `List<double>` | Yes | — | Gradient stops across the band. The first and last carry [backgroundColor]; every stop between them carries [waveColor], so the band fades in and out of the surface rather than ending on an edge. |
