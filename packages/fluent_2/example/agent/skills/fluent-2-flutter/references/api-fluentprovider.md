## Behavior and parity notes

Hand-audited behavior that no constructor signature can express. Treat a
documented gap as a real gap: do not describe it as working, and do not
work around it by inventing an API.

- Route transitions ignore reduced motion. `FluentApp` installs `FluentPageRoute` as its `pageRouteBuilder`, and that route's `buildTransitions` always fades and slides — nothing in `app.dart` reads `MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf`. Every named-route navigation therefore animates even when the platform asks for reduced motion, against this skill's own motion rule. Widget-level motion is unaffected; the gap is the route transition alone.
